Pasta Salad Recipe

A pasta salad with pepperoni, tomatoes, and provolone cheese that will have everyone wanting more. It's a very easy, light tasting side dish for a picnic or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package spiral pasta
  • 3 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 pound provolone cheese, cubed
  • 1/2 pound salami, cubed
  • 1/4 pound sliced pepperoni, cut in half
  • 1 large green bell pepper, cut into 1 inch pieces
  • 1 (10 ounce) can black olives, drained
  • 1 (4 ounce) jar pimentos, drained
  • 1 (8 ounce) bottle Italian salad dressing
  1. Boil pasta: In a large pot, bring lightly salted water to a boil. Add spiral pasta stirring occasionally and cook until soft yet firm to the bite (approx 12 minutes). Drain.
  2. Combine ingredients: In a large bowl combine pasta with tomatoes, cheese, salami, pepperoni, green pepper, olives and pimentos. Pour in salad dressing; toss and serve.
